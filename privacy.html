Privacy Policy

Clear data boundaries for a focused practice app.

Last updated: July 2026
Quick summary

What matters most

No account required for core use. No stored microphone recordings. Apple or Google may process payments.

Accounts

No mandatory profile system

Core use of FretMaster does not depend on creating an account or building a personal identity profile.

Microphone

Used for note recognition only

Microphone access exists to detect played notes. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to the developer.

Purchases

Handled through Apple or Google

In-app purchases may be processed by Apple or Google, and payment details are not stored by the developer.

Overview

FretMaster is a guitar learning app focused on note recognition, fretboard fluency, and efficient practice sessions.

Information collection

FretMaster does not collect personally identifiable information for core use of the app.

Microphone access

FretMaster requests microphone access only to detect notes played on a guitar. Audio is processed locally for note recognition and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to the developer.

In-app purchases

Premium features may be purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Payment processing may be handled by Apple or Google, and the developer does not receive or store payment information.

Third-party services

FretMaster may use third-party services to manage purchase entitlements and subscription state.
