Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Overview

FretMap is a guitar learning application designed to help users learn note locations on the guitar fretboard.

Information Collection

FretMap does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information.

Microphone Access

FretMap requests microphone access solely to detect notes played on a guitar. Audio is processed for note recognition and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to the developer.

In-App Purchases

Premium features may be purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment processing is handled by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment information.

Third-Party Services

FretMap may use Apple's services and RevenueCat to manage in-app purchase entitlements.
